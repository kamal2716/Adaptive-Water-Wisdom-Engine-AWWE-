ADAPTIVE WATER WISDOM ENGINE

An AI-powered decision-support platform that helps Madurai assess excess-rain capture, plan safe water storage, monitor water quality, and guide reuse during dry seasons. It connects traditional tank-cascade knowledge with data to reduce flood impacts and seasonal water scarcity.

Built for trust, not guesswork

Quantity is not the same as quality.

A tank holding water does not automatically mean that water is suitable for drinking or reliably available at a household tap. AWWE keeps storage, water quality, treatment, distribution and authorized decisions distinct.
